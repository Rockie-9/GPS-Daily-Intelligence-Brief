「晶片通膨」的本質是稀缺性定價。但安全領域有一個反直覺的真相：最有價值的守護，往往是「沒有事情發生」——沒有外洩、沒有中斷、沒有意外。這種價值難以量化，卻是台積電能持續收取溢價的隱形前提。GPS 的工作正是如此：當一切順利運作時，沒人會注意到安全的存在；但只要一次失守，代價就是整個價值鏈的中斷。這就是為什麼安全必須「在您需要之前」就到位。
"Chip inflation" is fundamentally scarcity pricing. But security holds a counterintuitive truth: the most valuable protection is often that "nothing happened" — no leak, no interruption, no incident. This value is hard to quantify, yet it's the invisible premise letting TSMC charge a premium. GPS's work is exactly this: when everything runs smoothly, no one notices security exists; but a single breach means interrupting the entire value chain. That's why security must be in place "before you need it."

「晶片通膨」時代：當稀缺性變成定價權，安全成為價值的守門人
The "chip inflation" era: when scarcity becomes pricing power, security guards the value

事實：市場傳出台積電將於 2026 下半年對 3nm 製程漲價達 15%，2027 年可能再漲 10%；先前亦傳 5nm 以下先進製程將連續漲價 4 年。先進封裝與記憶體（DRAM）同步喊漲。台經院產經資料庫總監劉佩真分析，主因是反映成本升高，並顯示先進製程已是「賣方市場」。台積電股價衝上 1525 元歷史新高、市值達 39.54 兆，貢獻大盤約 120 點。董事長魏哲家在 Q1 法說會稱 2 奈米是「半導體歷史上需求最強勁的節點」，客戶已在排隊。

為什麼重要：漲價的底層邏輯是「不可替代性」——只要你想要最先進的 AI 算力，就必須經過台積電這一關，這就是「收費站」的本質。但這也意味著：台積電的價值越高，它所承載的風險就越集中。當全世界的 AI 算力都依賴這一個節點，任何對這個節點的威脅——技術外洩、關鍵人才流失、實體中斷——都會被放大成系統性風險。

What happened: TSMC is reported to raise 3nm prices by up to 15% in H2 2026, possibly another 10% in 2027; earlier reports said sub-5nm advanced processes would rise for four consecutive years. Advanced packaging and DRAM are rising too. TIER's Liu Pei-chen attributes this to rising costs and advanced process being a "seller's market." TSMC shares hit a record NT$1,525, market cap NT$39.54T, contributing ~120 points to the index. Chairman Wei called 2nm "the strongest-demand node in semiconductor history" at the Q1 call, with customers queuing.

Why it matters: The underlying logic of the price hike is "irreplaceability" — if you want the most advanced AI compute, you must pass through TSMC; that's the essence of a "tollbooth." But it also means: the higher TSMC's value, the more concentrated the risk it bears. When the world's AI compute depends on this single node, any threat to it — tech leakage, key-talent loss, physical interruption — gets amplified into systemic risk.

GPS 解讀：對 GPS 而言，「晶片通膨」是一個提醒：我們守護的對象，其價值正在指數級上升。這改變了安全的「賭注」——過去一次安全事件可能是局部損失，現在可能是全球供應鏈的中斷。但安全有個反直覺的特性：它最大的成功，是「什麼都沒發生」。當台積電能持續收取溢價、客戶願意排隊，背後有一個隱形前提——這家公司的營運是穩定、可信、不會被中斷的。GPS 守的，正是這個「隱形前提」。
GPS Lens: For GPS, "chip inflation" is a reminder: the value of what we guard is rising exponentially. This changes the "stakes" of security — a past incident might have been a local loss; now it could interrupt the global supply chain. But security has a counterintuitive trait: its greatest success is that "nothing happened." When TSMC charges a premium and customers queue, there's an invisible premise — this company's operations are stable, trustworthy, uninterrupted. What GPS guards is precisely this "invisible premise."
建議行動：這不是要你今天做什麼，是換一個視角看 GPS 的價值定位：當公司市值與不可替代性同步攀升，安全的角色從「成本中心」轉變為「價值守護者」。下次跟人說明 GPS 在做什麼時，可以試試這個框架：「我們守的不是門禁與監控，是讓這家公司能持續被信任、被依賴的底層條件。」
Recommended action: This isn't asking you to act today; it's a reframe of GPS's value positioning: as market cap and irreplaceability rise together, security shifts from "cost center" to "value guardian." Next time you explain what GPS does, try this frame: "We don't guard gates and cameras — we guard the foundation that lets this company keep being trusted and relied upon."
可轉述句：「公司的市值會波動，但『能否穩定運作、持續被信任』這件事，是 GPS 每天在守的隱形價值。」
Cascade sentence: "Market cap fluctuates, but 'whether we can operate stably and stay trusted' is the invisible value GPS guards every day."

AI Governance Watch · Story 022 SOURCES
Google 示警「影子代理」：當員工自行部署 AI，繞過審批的隱形管道
Google warns of "shadow agents": when employees self-deploy AI, bypassing approval

事實：Google 發布《Cybersecurity Forecast 2026》報告指出，2026 年「影子 AI」將升級為更棘手的「影子代理（Shadow Agent）」危機——員工不再只是私下用 ChatGPT 查資料，而是獨立部署具自主執行能力的 AI 代理處理工作，完全繞過 IT 部門審批。這些不受控的代理成為企業內部的「隱形管道」，可能在毫無察覺下造成資料外洩。報告也預測「提示注入」攻擊成真、「代理式 SOC」興起、鏈上犯罪經濟崛起。

為什麼重要：「影子代理」的核心問題不是技術，是治理盲區——當員工為了效率自行引入 AI 工具，組織根本不知道有多少代理在存取什麼資料、執行什麼動作。這與實體安全的「未授權通道」概念高度相似：一扇沒人知道的後門，比正門的鎖更危險。對 GPS 而言，這提醒我們：安全的盲區，往往不在我們嚴防的地方，而在我們沒看見的地方。

What happened: Google's Cybersecurity Forecast 2026 warns that in 2026, "shadow AI" escalates into a thornier "shadow agent" crisis — employees no longer just privately use ChatGPT but independently deploy autonomous AI agents to handle work, fully bypassing IT approval. These uncontrolled agents become internal "invisible channels" that may cause data leaks unnoticed. The report also forecasts prompt-injection attacks turning real, the rise of "agentic SOCs," and an on-chain crime economy.

Why it matters: The core problem of "shadow agents" isn't technology but a governance blind spot — when employees self-introduce AI tools for efficiency, the organization has no idea how many agents access what data or perform what actions. This closely mirrors physical security's "unauthorized passage" concept: an unknown back door is more dangerous than a locked front gate. For GPS, this reminds us: security blind spots are often not where we guard tightly, but where we don't see.

GPS 解讀：GPS 雖然主責實體安全，但「影子代理」的治理邏輯完全適用於實體世界——有多少未登記的通道、多少被默許的便宜行事、多少「大家都這樣做」但從沒被正式盤點的流程？真正成熟的安全，不是把已知的門鎖得更緊，而是有系統地找出「我們不知道自己不知道」的盲區。GPS 內部若已開始使用 AI 工具自動化，更應以身作則建立「代理權限清冊」。
GPS Lens: Though GPS owns physical security, the governance logic of "shadow agents" fully applies to the physical world — how many unregistered passages, tacitly-allowed shortcuts, "everyone does it" processes never formally inventoried? Truly mature security isn't locking known doors tighter, but systematically finding the "we don't know what we don't know" blind spots. If GPS uses AI tools for automation internally, it should lead by example with an "agent privilege inventory."
建議行動：換一個視角做一次「盲區盤點」：在你負責的範圍內，有哪些「大家都這樣做、但從沒被正式記錄」的流程或通道？把它們列出來——不是要立刻禁止，是要先「看見」。最危險的風險，是那些習以為常到沒人會質疑的。

Industry Watch · Story 042 SOURCES
半導體業資安保險投保奪冠——成熟風險心態的標竿
Semiconductor leads in cyber insurance — a benchmark for mature risk mindset

事實：金管會統計，截至 2025 年底台灣資安保險保費收入達 5.12 億元，連續三年逾 5 億。其中半導體業投保金額約 1.48 億元、占比 28.87% 居冠，其次為電腦周邊（1.25 億）、金融保險（約 1 億）。2025 年理賠金額僅 793 萬元——業者指出企業投保前須經嚴格核保（資安防護、第三方稽核、營運持續計畫），已具一定防禦能力。

為什麼重要：半導體業在資安保險的領先，反映成熟的風險管理心態：不是「我們不會被攻擊」，而是「我們假設會被攻擊，並為殘餘風險做財務準備」。當「晶片通膨」讓這個產業的價值持續攀升，為殘餘風險做準備就更顯重要——價值越高，殘餘風險的代價也越大。

What happened: Taiwan's FSC reports cyber insurance premiums reached NT$512M by end-2025, exceeding NT$500M for three years. Semiconductor leads at ~NT$148M (28.87%), then computer peripherals (NT$125M) and finance (~NT$100M). 2025 claims totaled only NT$7.93M — insurers note firms must pass rigorous underwriting (security measures, third-party audits, business continuity plans) before coverage.

Why it matters: Semiconductor's insurance lead reflects a mature risk mindset: not "we won't be attacked," but "we assume we will be, and prepare financially for residual risk." As "chip inflation" keeps raising the industry's value, preparing for residual risk matters more — the higher the value, the greater the cost of residual risk.

GPS 解讀：借用「保險核保邏輯」做一次 GPS 自我盤點：如果有一份「實體安全保險」要為我們的廠區核保，核保人會問什麼？（門禁完整性、監控覆蓋率、應變計畫、稽核紀錄）把這些問題列出來，本身就是一份 GPS 成熟度自評表。這框架對主管層特別有說服力，因為它用財務語言講安全。
GPS Lens: Borrow "underwriting logic" for a GPS self-assessment: if a "physical security insurance" underwrote our sites, what would the underwriter ask? (Access integrity, surveillance coverage, response plans, audit records.) Listing these is itself a GPS maturity self-assessment — especially persuasive to leadership because it speaks security in financial language.
建議行動：換一個視角看資產保護：保險業用「核保問卷」量化風險，GPS 也可以。試著想像一份「實體安全核保問卷」會問什麼——門禁、監控、應變、稽核。能回答得越完整，代表 GPS 成熟度越高。缺口就是下一季的改善方向。
Recommended action: Reframe asset protection: insurers quantify risk via "underwriting questionnaires" — GPS can too. 可轉述句：「成熟的安全不是承諾零風險，是誠實面對殘餘風險並為它做準備——價值越高，這份準備越重要。」
Cascade sentence: "Mature security isn't promising zero risk — it's honestly facing residual risk and preparing for it; the higher the value, the more this preparation matters."

People Safety Watch · Story 052 SOURCES
假冒主管 LINE 詐騙在職場擴散——查證文化是最好的防線
Fake-boss LINE scams spread in workplaces — a verification culture is the best defense

事實：內政部 165 反詐騙專線多次發布警示，假冒公司主管的 LINE 訊息詐騙在職場持續擴散：詐騙者冒用主管名義建立 LINE 群組或私訊員工，以「急需匯款」、「代購禮品卡」、「處理機密交易」等話術誘騙財務或行政人員。手法關鍵在於利用組織層級壓力——員工接到「主管」指示時，傾向先服從、後查證。趨勢科技亦預測 2026 年 AI 詐騙將更快速、更客製化。

為什麼重要：詐騙能得逞，根因不是員工不夠聰明，是組織文化讓人不敢質疑權威。這是 GPS Pillar 06 人身安全最貼近員工日常的場景，也是「文化」而非「技術」的問題——正是 GPS 理解組織文化的優勢所在。當 AI 讓詐騙更逼真，唯一不變的防線是「查證的習慣」。

What happened: Taiwan's 165 anti-fraud hotline repeatedly warns as fake-boss LINE scams spread in workplaces: scammers impersonate executives via LINE groups or DMs, using scripts like "urgent transfer," "buy gift cards," "handle a confidential transaction" to deceive finance or admin staff. The key is exploiting hierarchy pressure — when employees receive instructions from "an executive," they comply first, verify later. Trend Micro also forecasts faster, more customized AI scams in 2026.

Why it matters: Scams succeed not because employees aren't smart, but because culture makes them afraid to question authority. This is the scenario closest to daily life under GPS Pillar 06 Personal Safety, and a "culture" not "technical" problem — exactly where GPS's cultural understanding is an advantage. As AI makes scams more convincing, the one unchanging defense is "the habit of verifying."

GPS 解讀：真正的安全文化，是讓「查證」變成被鼓勵、而非被質疑的行為。製作一張極簡「停看聽」卡片：收到主管異常指示（匯款、買卡、機密交易）時，停（正常公務不會用私人 LINE 急迫要錢）、看（是否有壓力話術）、聽（用另一個已知管道向本人查證）。關鍵訊息：「查證不會冒犯主管，被騙才會。」
GPS Lens: A real safety culture makes "verifying" an encouraged act, not a doubted one. Create a minimal "Stop-Look-Listen" card: on receiving abnormal executive instructions (transfers, cards, confidential transactions), Stop (normal business won't urgently demand money via personal LINE), Look (any pressure scripts?), Listen (verify with the person via another known channel). Key message: "Verifying won't offend the executive — being scammed will."
建議行動：這個觀念可以變成行動：把「停看聽」做成一頁卡片，推送給財務、行政、採購等高風險族群。卡片不需長，核心是給員工一個「不會被究責的查證理由」。
Recommended action: This idea can become action: turn "Stop-Look-Listen" into a one-page card, push to high-risk groups like finance, admin, procurement. The card need not be long — its core is giving employees a "blame-free reason to verify."
可轉述句：「我們提醒同仁查證，不是因為不信任主管——是因為我們知道，真正的主管永遠不介意被查證。會介意的，才是該擔心的。」
Cascade sentence: "We remind colleagues to verify not because we distrust executives — but because a real executive never minds being verified. The ones who mind are the ones to worry about."
