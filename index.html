當最弱環節是最受信任的人，GPS 守的是「設計進制度的信任」
When the weakest link is the most trusted person, GPS guards 'trust designed into the system'

台灣智財及商業法院於 2026 年 4 月對一起 2 奈米先進製程營業秘密案做出判決：多名工程師因竊取、洩漏製程機密遭判 2 至 10 年不等刑期；協助接收資料的一家國際半導體設備供應商遭判罰金 1.5 億元、緩刑三年。媒體分析指出，此案確立三大新紅線——偵審加速（約 9 個月內定讞）、刑度加重、以及「受讓方企業連帶責任」：企業不只要防止自家機密被盜，更要確保不去接收、使用他人竊取的機密。案件起點，是一名離職轉往供應商的前員工，委託在職前同事翻拍製程資訊傳遞——典型的「內部人 × 供應鏈」複合風險。
In April 2026, Taiwan's IP & Commercial Court ruled on a 2nm advanced-process trade-secret case: several engineers received 2-to-10-year sentences for stealing and leaking process secrets, while an international semiconductor-equipment supplier that received the data was fined NT$150 million with a three-year suspended term. Analysts note the case sets three new red lines — expedited investigation and trial (a verdict within roughly nine months), harsher sentencing, and 'recipient-company liability': a firm must not only prevent its own secrets from being stolen, but also ensure it never receives or uses secrets stolen from others. The case began with a former employee who moved to the supplier and asked an in-house ex-colleague to photograph process information — a textbook 'insider × supply-chain' compound risk.
為什麼這是今日主信號：這個判決把「內部人威脅」從抽象風險變成具體判例，最深刻的啟示有二。第一，最危險的破口未必來自外部攻擊，而是來自有合法權限、被信任的內部人——他們不需要突破任何防線，因為防線本來就為他們敞開。第二，責任邊界擴大了：在 AI 與人才高度流動的產業，企業不只要當被害者的防範方，也可能在不知情中成為「受讓方」。對任何管理門禁、權限與人員進出的組織，這都是直接的治理命題。
Why this is today's lead: This verdict turns 'insider threat' from an abstract risk into concrete case law, with two deep lessons. First, the most dangerous gap may not come from external attack but from a trusted insider with legitimate access — they need not breach any defense, because it was open to them by design. Second, the liability boundary has widened: in an industry of AI and high talent mobility, a firm must guard not only as a potential victim, but against unknowingly becoming a 'recipient.' For any organization managing access, privileges, and personnel movement, this is a direct governance question.
GPS 透鏡
GPS LENS
對 GPS 而言，內部人威脅的答案不是「更不信任同仁」，而是更好的「設計」。深守的節制者不把任何人當嫌疑犯，而是把信任做成可被治理的結構：誰能進入哪些區域、誰能接觸哪些資料、權限在離職時是否確實回收、敏感空間的進出是否留下紀錄？這些都不是監控，而是「安全設計」——讓正直的人更容易做對的事，讓制度而非人情承擔守護的重量。
For GPS, the answer to insider threat isn't 'trust colleagues less' but better 'design.' The steward with depth treats no one as a suspect, but makes trust a governable structure: who can enter which zones, who can access which data, whether privileges are truly reclaimed at departure, whether entry to sensitive spaces leaves a record? None of this is surveillance — it is 'designed safety,' making it easier for honest people to do the right thing, and letting the system rather than personal favors carry the weight of protection.
可轉述句
「最受信任的人不需要破壞防線，因為防線為他敞開——真正的守護，是把信任設計成可治理的結構，而非寄望於人人自律。」
Cascade sentence
"The most trusted person needn't breach the defense, because it was open to them — real protection designs trust into a governable structure, rather than hoping everyone self-polices."

2 奈米竊密案判決：內部人威脅，從抽象風險變成具體判例
2nm trade-secret verdict: insider threat moves from abstract risk to case law

事實：台灣智財及商業法院於 2026 年 4 月對一起 2 奈米先進製程營業秘密案做出判決：多名工程師因竊取、洩漏製程機密遭判 2 至 10 年不等刑期；協助接收資料的一家國際半導體設備供應商遭判罰金 1.5 億元、緩刑三年（條件包含支付被害企業與國庫共計 1.5 億元）。檢方僅約一個月即掌握事證起訴，並在 2025 年 12 月、2026 年 1 月兩度追加起訴，全案在 9 個月內定讞。媒體分析指出，此案確立三大法律新紅線：偵審加速、刑度加重、以及最受矚目的「受讓方企業連帶責任」——企業不只要防止自家機密被盜，更要確保自己不去接收、使用他人竊取的機密。

為什麼重要：這個判決之所以是今日主信號，在於它把「內部人威脅」從教科書名詞變成了有刑期、有罰金的真實後果。案件起點，是一名離職轉往設備供應商的前員工，委託仍在職的前同事翻拍製程資訊傳遞——這是「內部人 × 供應鏈」的複合風險：最危險的破口，來自一個曾經被完全信任、握有合法權限的人。他不需要突破任何防線，因為防線本來就為他敞開。

What happened: In April 2026, Taiwan's IP & Commercial Court ruled on a 2nm advanced-process trade-secret case: several engineers received 2-to-10-year sentences for stealing and leaking process secrets; an international semiconductor-equipment supplier that received the data was fined NT$150 million with a three-year suspended term (conditioned on paying the victim firm and the treasury a combined NT$150M). Prosecutors built the case in about a month, added charges twice (Dec 2025, Jan 2026), and reached a verdict within nine months. Analysts note three new legal red lines: expedited trials, harsher sentencing, and the closely-watched "recipient-company liability" — a firm must not only prevent its own secrets from being stolen, but ensure it never receives or uses secrets stolen from others.

Why it matters: This verdict is today's lead because it turns "insider threat" from a textbook term into real consequences with prison terms and fines. The case began with a former employee who moved to the equipment supplier and asked an in-house ex-colleague to photograph process information — an "insider × supply-chain" compound risk: the most dangerous gap comes from someone once fully trusted, holding legitimate access. They needed not breach any defense, because it was open to them by design.

GPS 解讀：對 GPS 而言，內部人威脅的答案不是「更不信任同仁」，而是更好的「設計」。深守的節制者不把任何人當嫌疑犯，而是把「信任」做成可被治理的結構：誰能進入哪些區域、誰能接觸哪些敏感資料、權限在角色變動或離職時是否確實回收、敏感空間的進出是否留下可追溯的紀錄。這些都不是監控，而是「安全設計」——讓正直的人更容易做對的事，讓制度而非人情承擔守護的重量。最好的內控，是讓同仁感覺被支持，而不是被懷疑。
GPS Lens: For GPS, the answer to insider threat isn't "trust colleagues less" but better "design." The steward with depth treats no one as a suspect; instead it makes "trust" a governable structure: who can enter which zones, who can access which sensitive data, whether privileges are truly reclaimed at role-change or departure, whether entry to sensitive spaces leaves a traceable record. None of this is surveillance — it is "designed safety," making it easier for honest people to do the right thing, and letting the system rather than personal favors carry the weight of protection. The best internal control makes colleagues feel supported, not suspected.
建議行動：用「角色變動」這個時間點檢視一次：當一位同仁轉調、升遷或離職，他原本的實體門禁權限、敏感區域進出資格、設備借用權，是否有一個明確的流程在當天就同步調整？權限的「給予」通常很順暢，但「回收」往往被忽略。把離職與轉調的權限回收，做成一張不需任何人記憶的檢核表——這就是把信任設計進制度。
Recommended action: Review around the moment of "role change": when a colleague transfers, is promoted, or departs, is there a clear process that adjusts their physical access, sensitive-area entry, and equipment privileges the same day? Granting access is usually smooth; reclaiming it is often overlooked. Turn privilege-reclamation at departure and transfer into a checklist no one has to remember — that is trust designed into the system.
可轉述句：「最受信任的人不需要破壞防線，因為防線為他敞開——真正的守護，是把信任設計成可治理的結構，而非寄望於人人自律。」
Cascade sentence: "The most trusted person needn't breach the defense, because it was open to them — real protection designs trust into a governable structure, rather than hoping everyone self-polices."

A process's value isn't in catching wrongdoing or breeding distrust — it's in ensuring every privilege (access, sensitive-area entry, data access) has a clear trail at grant, change, and reclamation, especially in the high-risk window of role change.
→ Natural collaborators: cross-functional partners (HR, Legal) are indispensable here.

08a · 今日一個策略思考
08a · Today's Strategic Reflection
最危險的破口，是被您完全信任的那個人
The most dangerous gap is the person you completely trust

2 奈米竊密案給所有人一個提醒：最大的風險，未必來自外部攻擊，而是來自有合法權限、被信任的內部人——他們不需要破壞防線，因為防線本來就為他們敞開。但這不代表要去懷疑身邊每個人。換個視角看您的工作範圍：哪些敏感的接觸、複製、攜出，目前是靠「我相信他不會」在守，而不是靠制度在守？把信任設計成結構，不是因為不信任人，而是讓正直的人不必獨自承擔被誤解的風險。

The 2nm trade-secret case offers everyone a reminder: the greatest risk may not come from external attack, but from a trusted insider with legitimate access — they needn't breach the defense, because it was open to them by design. But this doesn't mean suspecting everyone around you. Reframe your scope: which sensitive access, copying, or carrying-out is currently guarded by "I trust they won't" rather than by the system? Designing trust into structure isn't about distrusting people — it lets honest people avoid bearing the risk of being misunderstood alone.
